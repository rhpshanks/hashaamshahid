Case study LIVE DAILY · MIQYAS.PK

Miqyas: a news service that researches, writes and publishes itself

Every morning it reads Pakistani news sources, writes an original summary of each story in its own words, scores the likely economic, political and social impact, merges duplicate coverage of the same event, and publishes the finished site — without me opening a laptop.

2+ hoursWhat the same work took by hand, every single day
Under 5 minMy actual daily effort now, and only to review
9:00 AMRuns itself on schedule, publishes with no prompt from me

What had to happen daily

  • Read every source and spot which stories cover the same event
  • Write an original summary of each one, never copied text
  • Judge and tag the impact of each story across three measures
  • Publish the site and confirm the update actually went live

What I built instead

  • A scheduled agent that reads, writes, scores and files each morning
  • Safe reruns: running twice in a day merges, it never overwrites
  • Rules that flag risky stories for a human and admit low confidence
  • Publishing wired to deploy automatically, so nothing waits on me

The same shape as most business automation: a task that is genuinely daily, mostly judgement, and expensive precisely because a person has to be there for it.

Questions

The things people ask first

Will this get my WhatsApp number banned?+

Not the way I build it. I use the official WhatsApp Business API through a Meta-approved provider. Plenty of cheaper automation runs on unofficial tools that scrape or spoof the app — those get numbers banned, and when your business runs on that number, that's not a small problem. If someone quotes you far less, this is usually why.

Do I need to change the tools we already use?+

Usually not. Most businesses here run on WhatsApp, Excel or Google Sheets, and maybe one accounting package. That's normally enough to automate against. I'd rather wire up what your team already knows than sell you a migration you didn't ask for.

Is this just ChatGPT with extra steps?+

Some of it uses language models — reading a messy invoice, understanding an order written in Roman Urdu, drafting a reply. Plenty of it doesn't need AI at all and shouldn't use it. The audit tells you which is which. Anything that has to be exactly right every time gets built on rules, not a model's best guess.

What does it cost to run each month?+

WhatsApp charges per conversation, and the rate depends on whether the customer or your business starts it. For most small businesses this lands in the low thousands of rupees monthly, but it scales with volume. I'll give you a real estimate from your actual message numbers during the audit, before you commit to anything.

What if it breaks after you've handed it over?+

Thirty days of fixes are included after launch. After that you can hire me for changes as you need them, or your own team can maintain it — the documentation and the accounts are yours, in your name, not locked inside my agency account.
