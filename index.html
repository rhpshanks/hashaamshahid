What just happened

Every reply came from a small set of facts about the clinic — opening hours, price list, address, which dentist does what. No human was involved, and nothing you type leaves your browser.

  • 01It reads what you meantAsk “kitna”, “how much” or “charges” and you get the price list. Misspell it and it still understands.
  • 02It sounds like a personShort sentences, no menus, no “press 1 for reception”. It even mistypes occasionally and corrects itself.
  • 03It finishes the jobAsk to book and it asks for a day, offers real slots, then confirms with the address and how to cancel.
  • 04It knows when to stopAsk something outside its knowledge and it says so, then tells you when a human will pick up. Guessing is what damages trust.
